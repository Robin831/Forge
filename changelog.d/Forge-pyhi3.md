category: Fixed
- **Consolidation no longer produces immediately-sweepable rules** - A merged Warden rule now carries its own `merged_at` date, and the staleness sweep measures the rule's age from that rather than from the `added` date it inherited. Consolidation exists to collapse old near-duplicates, so a cluster whose members were all past `archive_after_days` produced a survivor that was born stale and was archived by the very next sweep — with every member it stood in for already in the archive, so the file lost the coverage with nothing left on it to say what went. A merged rule's `added` is now the newest member's rather than the oldest, and its usage telemetry (last emission, summed emit count) continues to carry forward across the merge. (Forge-pyhi3)
- **Learned rules no longer carry model-supplied merge or usage stamps** - Both Warden learners unmarshal the model's raw JSON into a rule, so a `merged_at`, `last_emitted`, `emit_count` or `last_finding` key in that answer landed on the rule verbatim. Since the staleness sweep anchors a rule's age on `merged_at` and its activity on the usage stamps, an invented date there made a rule permanently un-sweepable. Those four fields are now cleared at both learner sites, alongside the source, added date and paths that were already derived rather than trusted. (Forge-pyhi3)
