category: Added
- **Kiln preview environments wired into the daemon** - The daemon now owns the Kiln manager: it is built only when `preview_enabled` is on globally and at least one anvil has not opted out via its own `preview_enabled`, reconciles previews orphaned by a previous daemon lifetime before IPC/web handlers accept traffic, runs the idle reaper under the daemon's run context, and tears every live preview down on shutdown so no worktree or process group leaks. With previews disabled the manager stays nil and all preview paths degrade to no-ops. (Forge-bpd9)
- **Previews are released when their PR closes** - A merged or closed PR now automatically tears down the preview for its bead, freeing the worktree, the ports and the concurrency slot instead of holding them until the idle timeout. (Forge-bpd9)
