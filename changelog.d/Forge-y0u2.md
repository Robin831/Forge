category: Changed
- **Assay per-pass turn telemetry now counts what the budget counts** - The `turns=` field in the daemon's Assay log line carried the provider's `num_turns`, which counts tool-result rounds rather than the model messages `--max-turns` actually caps: it overstated 38% of successful sessions (by up to 7) and reported a constant cap+1 for every session the budget killed. Assay now counts the distinct model messages off the session stream, falling back to the provider's figure for backends that stamp no message ids. (Forge-y0u2)
- **Assay default turn budget raised from 12 to 16** - Over 451 logged sessions, 7.1% of all passes (11.5% of tool-using ones) died at the 12-turn cap, each one paying for a full session plus a retry and often still reporting partial coverage. A turn budget is a clip point rather than an allowance, so the raise costs nothing for the 69% of sessions that answer in five turns or fewer, and `assay.max_cost_per_pass_usd` now bounds a genuine runaway on spend. Set `assay.max_turns_per_pass` per anvil for a repo that needs more; see `docs/assay-turn-budget.md` for the analysis behind the number. (Forge-y0u2)
- **A negative `assay.max_turns_per_pass` is now rejected** - It reads like a switch and silently did nothing; config validation names the block that wrote it. `0` still means "use the engine default". (Forge-y0u2)
