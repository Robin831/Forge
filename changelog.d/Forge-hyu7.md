category: Changed
- **Ledger: anvil-first navigation** - The Ledger now opens an anvil picker instead of loading all beads at startup. Top-level shows registered anvils; selecting one fetches that anvil's beads. Closed beads are hidden by default; press `c` to toggle visibility. Press `esc` or `f` to return to the anvil picker from any bead view. (Forge-hyu7)
