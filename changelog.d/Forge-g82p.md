- **Hearth frontend persistence tests no longer hang on a frozen clock** - The QueuePane, WorkersPane, LiveActivity and PRsPage back-navigation tests held vitest fake timers installed across `router.navigate()`; React's async `act()` and react-router both yield to the macrotask queue, which nothing advances while the clock is frozen, so a navigation could never settle and the test died on its 5s timeout. The fake clock is now scoped to the one rAF + debounce flush that needs it via a shared `captureScrollWithFakeTimers` helper, and QueuePane gained the `afterEach` real-timer reset its sibling suites already had.
