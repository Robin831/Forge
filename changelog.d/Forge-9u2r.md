category: Fixed
- **Temper steps are bounded by their deadline** - A step's cancellation now kills the whole process group and stops waiting on the stdout/stderr pipes an orphaned descendant is holding open, so a command that spawns a longer-lived child (`npm run lint` -> `cross-env` -> `eslint`) can no longer outlive its timeout — one was measured running 168.7s past a hard deadline. (Forge-9u2r)
- **A Temper step killed by its deadline is no longer reported as a failing one** - It is recorded as terminated, rendered `TIMEOUT` rather than `FAIL` in the summary and the temper log, and its retained output says the exit status is the kill and not the command's verdict — a lint that passed cleanly had been reported FAIL with its own clean report underneath. A killed step is also never tolerated into a PASS by the .NET host-crash carve-out, and a step ended by a cancelled parent is classified `infra` rather than `test_failure`. (Forge-9u2r)
