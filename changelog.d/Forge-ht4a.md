category: Changed
- **TypeScript 7 in the Hearth frontend** - Bumped the Hearth 2.0 frontend to typescript 7.0.2 (from 6.0.3), completing the batch of deferred frontend major upgrades alongside @testing-library/jest-dom 7, @types/node 26 and jsdom 30. TypeScript 7 is the native compiler port, so it resolves through per-platform optional dependencies — all 20 platform binaries are pinned in package-lock.json to keep `npm ci` reproducible on Linux, macOS and Windows CI. Typecheck, the 462-test vitest suite and the vite build all pass, and the embedded internal/web/dist bundle rebuilds byte-identical (tsc runs with noEmit; vite/esbuild does the transpiling). (Forge-ht4a)
