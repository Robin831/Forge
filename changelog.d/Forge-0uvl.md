category: Changed
- **Depcheck blocked-scan escalations say what to do** - A blocked dependency scan no longer reports git's raw stderr as its headline. The Needs Attention entry now names the anvil and its checkout, enumerates the blocking paths from `git status` — annotating the pod-local `.beads/config.yaml` and bd-regenerated `.beads/.gitignore` so an operator recognises them as expected rather than as the problem — and gives the single recoverable command that resolves the condition — bounded honestly, so where more paths are blocking than fit one readable command the line says it sets the first five aside and points at `git status` for the rest, rather than reading as the whole fix. git's own output is kept as the trailing `git output:` evidence. Which condition is escalated, and when it is suppressed, is unchanged. (Forge-0uvl)
