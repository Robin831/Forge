- **A ready bead the anvil would auto-dispatch says why it is not dispatched** - The dispatcher skipped such a bead without a word when it was flagged needs_human or clarification_needed in the Forge's state, already in flight, held by a full anvil or global cap, or seen as having an open PR. A Munin bead was counted as ready on every poll for eleven hours while a needs_human flag from an escalation held it back. Each of these skips now writes one INFO line, `ready bead not dispatched`, with the reason. A full anvil names the workers holding it, and a flagged bead gives since when, how long, and the error that set the flag. The line is written when the reason changes, not on every poll. Beads the anvil would not auto-dispatch anyway are not logged. (DevInfra-jh9)
