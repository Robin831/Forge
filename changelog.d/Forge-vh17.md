category: Fixed
- **Bellows monitor rows survive a daemon restart** - Shutdown's force-kill phase and startup orphan cleanup no longer mark bellows' synthetic `bellows-<anvil>-<n>` PR-monitor rows failed: they hold no process and no work, and `InsertWorkerIfMissing` could never undo the marking, so an open PR stayed failed for its whole life and vanished from the Bellows lane while bellows was still managing it. The bellows poll loop now also revives a monitor row it finds in a terminal status while its PR is open, so the recovery is self-healing regardless of who marked the row. (Forge-vh17)
