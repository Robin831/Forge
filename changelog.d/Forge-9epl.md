category: Added
- **Kiln preview manifest and settings** - New `internal/kiln` package reads a project's `.forge/preview.yaml` (services, ports, health checks, setup/teardown) from the anvil's main checkout and expands `{{.Port}}`, `{{.ServicePort "name"}}`, `{{.PreviewID}}` and `{{.Host}}` templates, with validation that names the offending service and field. Adds the `preview_enabled`, `preview_max_concurrent`, `preview_idle_timeout`, `preview_port_range`, `preview_bind_host` and `preview_public_host` settings plus the per-anvil `preview_enabled` tri-state override; previews are off by default and no preview runtime ships yet. (Forge-9epl)
