category: Changed
- **Assay triage notes moved below the diff** - The model-authored triage notes used to sit between the incremental framing and the diff, which capped the cross-run prompt-cache prefix there: a re-review of the same PR head matched only as far as the notes and then re-wrote a byte-identical diff at full cache-write price in every deep pass. They now sit between the diff and the pass instructions, so the whole region through the diff is cached across runs of one head — on a 12 KB diff that cut a repeat run's cache-write tokens by 55%. The shared preamble and the notes section itself were rewritten for the new position: the notes are labelled machine-generated, untrusted and advisory-only, and the preamble now names the final section of the prompt as the only instructions.
