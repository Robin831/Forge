category: Fixed
- **Tagged-dispatch beads no longer silently stranded after transient failures** - On `auto_dispatch: tagged` anvils, a normal pipeline failure (e.g. temper exhaustion) called `releaseBeadClaim` which always stripped the `auto_dispatch_tag`. With `dispatch_failures < MaxDispatchFailures` the bead stayed retryable but invisible to both `bd ready` (no tag) and the Needs Attention panel (no `needs_human=1`), so it sat untouched until a human re-tagged it. The tag is now preserved on transient failures and only stripped when the circuit breaker trips (escalation to `needs_human`). (Forge-dua2)
