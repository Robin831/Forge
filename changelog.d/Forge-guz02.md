category: Added
- **Warden rule usage telemetry** - Learned review rules now record when they were last rendered into a review checklist and how many times (`last_emitted`/`emit_count`, plus a `last_finding` field carried for future finding attribution), stamped by the Warden's own emission path and persisted in the anvil's `.forge/warden-rules.yaml`. `Rule.LastActivity()` folds the three dates into the most recent evidence a rule is alive. All three fields are optional, so existing rules files load and re-save unchanged, and an absent value means "never observed" rather than "stale". (Forge-guz02)
