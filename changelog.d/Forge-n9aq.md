category: Fixed
- **Warden review rules are now selected, not truncated in file order** - The `max_rules_per_review` cap took the first N matching rules in file order, and rules are appended as they are learned, so it returned the oldest surviving rules and only ever those — on this repository's own 727-rule file across its 841 merged PRs, 125 rules were reachable in total and only 4 of them post-dated May. Candidates are now ranked by path specificity, how much of the rule's pattern vocabulary the diff actually contains, and recency, with ties broken on content rather than position, so the emitted checklist no longer depends on the order of the file: 369 rules reachable over the same PRs, every rule learned since May among them. (Forge-n9aq)
- **The pattern filter needs more than one word** - A rule was kept when any single ≥4-character word from its pattern appeared anywhere in the diff, which a pattern written in ordinary English nearly always satisfies — so the filter passed almost everything and the cap performed the whole selection. Two distinct words are now required (a pattern too short to have two is held to the words it does have), and the hit count feeds the ranking. (Forge-n9aq)
- **Each review logs its rule funnel** - `rules funnel: 727 on file, 512 after paths, 498 after category, 431 matched, 30 emitted (cap 30)`, because a checklist of 30 reads identically whether 30 candidates survived the filters or 431 did. (Forge-n9aq)
- **The learned rules file is bounded** - New `settings.warden.max_rules_in_file` (default 1000, negative disables) makes the Smelter evict the lowest-value rules — least specific paths, oldest — into the archive store with reason `over-cap` once the active file grows past the ceiling. (Forge-n9aq)
