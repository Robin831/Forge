category: Fixed
- **Kiln reports a preview service that dies after startup** - Health was one-shot readiness: a service that passed its check and later exited kept reading `healthy`, with a growing uptime, on the preview panel, in `forge preview list` and in the previews payload. The supervisor's observation of the exit is now consumed — the service goes to a new `exited` state with its exit code and the lifetime it had, its uptime freezes at the death, and the preview's status is recomputed by the same rule that ran at startup (`degraded` while something still serves, `failed` when nothing does). (Forge-bci1)
- **No preview link to a dead entry service** - An entry service that failed or has exited now withholds the preview URL and says why, on the panel, in `forge preview list` and through the host-based proxy (which answers "no longer running" instead of a connection error). The link is withheld in both addressing forms, so a deployment with `preview_proxy_base` set no longer keeps offering the preview hostname beside the note explaining why there is no link. Handing out the address of a dead process produced a browser error that read as a broken tunnel rather than a dead service. (Forge-bci1)
- **`preview_service_exited` event** - A preview service dying after readiness is recorded in the activity feed with the service, its exit code, how long it lived and the preview's new status, so a limb dying is audible without opening the panel. Services killed by teardown or shutdown are not reported — that death is the stop working. (Forge-bci1)
