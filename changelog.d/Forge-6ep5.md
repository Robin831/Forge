- **Assay daily cost cap accounts for reviews in flight** - The cap was compared against `assay_runs`, where a row appears only when a run ends, so concurrent reviews carried the day past the limit (2026-08-19: $185.45 against a $150 limit). A review is now admitted only if recorded spend, the estimates held for the reviews already running, and one estimate for itself all fit under the cap; the estimate is the larger of the rolling mean of recent recorded runs and the new `assay.run_cost_estimate_usd` floor (default $1.00), bounded above by the daily cap so an estimate no day could satisfy can never refuse every review. A review whose budget is spoken for by running ones is deferred until they finish and — unlike a genuinely exhausted day — its head is not released to merge readiness. (Forge-6ep5)
