category: Fixed
- **A worker whose process is gone is failed, not stalled** - The stale detector now checks that the process a silent worker row records is still running before it applies the `stalled` mask. `stalled` is recoverable and is only ever cleared by fresh log writes from the very session that has died, so a crashed or externally killed worker held its `max_total_smiths` slot forever — on a single-slot forge that stopped dispatch entirely with nothing in the log but the same "limit reached" line every poll. Such a row is now marked `failed` (emitting `worker_process_gone`) and the slot is released within one detector interval. The check is confined to the `smith` and `schematic` phases — the two that write the pid column — since a row in `temper` or `warden` carries the pid of the Smith that finished before it by design; rows handed off live to Bellows (`monitoring`), operator-paused rows, and rows recording no pid also keep the previous behaviour. (Forge-e6ir)
- **The global Smith limit says when one worker has been holding it** - `global smith limit reached, skipping dispatch` reads identically whether a busy forge is turning its slots over every cycle or one wedged worker has held the same slot since Tuesday. The daemon now tracks *which* workers occupy the limit across polls and adds a WARN naming the blocking worker, its bead, phase and age once the same one has held it for more than `settings.wedged_limit_polls` consecutive polls (default 3; negative disables). Ordinary saturation keeps its unchanged INFO line, and a wedged holder is announced once per run rather than every poll interval. (Forge-e6ir)
