category: Added
- **Auto-close a parent bead when its last child closes** - In independent mode (any parent without the `crucible` opt-in) nothing closed a parent once its children merged one by one, so it stayed open forever and blocked whatever was queued behind it. After a post-merge `bd close` lands, Forge now resolves the child's parents and closes one whose children bd reports as *all* closed, walking up to five levels and recording a `parent_bead_auto_closed` event. Orchestrated parents are left to the Crucible, and every unreadable answer — bd down, an unparseable payload, a child whose status is not `closed` — leaves the parent open. Opt out with `settings.auto_close_parents: false`. (Forge-jniq)
