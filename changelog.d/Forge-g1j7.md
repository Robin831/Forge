category: Fixed
- **Leaked worker rows from a crashed or killed daemon are reaped** - Worker rows whose owning goroutine died with the daemon (crash, SIGKILL, OOM, reboot) stayed non-terminal forever, counting against `max_total_smiths` and holding a panel open for a process that was gone. The daemon now stamps each row with the lifetime that inserted it (`workers.daemon_generation`) and heartbeats the rows it is running (`workers.heartbeat_at`), and ends the rows nothing owns at startup and every 10 minutes. The evidence is ownership rather than PID liveness — a recycled PID reads alive and a row's PID is dead by design for every phase past the spawn — and `paused` rows are excluded structurally, so a parked bead whose session is still resumable is never touched. (Forge-g1j7)
