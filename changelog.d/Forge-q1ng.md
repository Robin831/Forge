category: Changed
- **Stop emitting per-poll success events** - The daemon no longer writes a `poll` row to the events table on every successful anvil poll; with multiple anvils on a ~30s cycle this was producing hundreds of rows per hour and drowning interesting events (smith_done, warden_pass, ci_failed, etc.) in `forge history events` and the Hearth/Mezzanine events panels. Failed polls continue to be persisted as `poll_error` so failures stay visible. Per-anvil "Last Poll" timestamps are now tracked in-memory on the daemon and exposed via the existing `status` IPC command, so `forge status` and the Hearth/Mezzanine indicators read freshness without hitting SQLite. (Forge-q1ng)
