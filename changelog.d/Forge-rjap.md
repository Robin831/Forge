category: Added
- **Grouping parents close themselves** - With epic orchestration opt-in, a parent bead filed purely to group work is dispatched independently and nothing closed it once its children were done. The merge path now closes such a parent after the last child's close lands, naming the children in the close reason and logging a `bead_auto_closed` event. Parents that opted into the Crucible (`crucible` / `epic-branch:<name>`) are left alone — the Crucible closes those — and any bd lookup that fails leaves the parent open. (Forge-rjap)
