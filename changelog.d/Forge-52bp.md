- **Dispatch always finalises its worker row** - A dispatch goroutine that exited by a path with no status update of its own — the pipeline's abort when a Temper step is killed by a cancelled context, or a panic unwinding the stack — left its worker row claiming a running Smith, so the row counted against `max_total_smiths` forever and kept a live panel open for a process that was gone. A deferred backstop now marks such a row failed at every exit and records a `worker_abandoned` event naming the status it replaced. It is idempotent with the roughly thirty existing termination sites (the decision is made in the UPDATE's own WHERE clause) and never touches a row that was handed off live on purpose: `monitoring`/`detached`, which Bellows owns after a successful dispatch, or `paused`, which a cold resume owns. (Forge-52bp)
