- **Warden rules are placed where their evidence is** - A learned rule's `paths` are now derived from the files that actually taught it and scoped to their top-level area (`api/**/*.cs`), instead of the bare `**/*.cs` that fires on every diff of that language. The learner (Copilot comments and CI-fix distillation) and the Smelter's Pass 3 backfill now run the one same derivation (`warden.DeriveRulePaths`), so a rule learned from a code-only change no longer carries `**/*.md`, no longer joins the review checklist on documentation-only diffs, and is no longer re-placed by the next backfill it meets.
