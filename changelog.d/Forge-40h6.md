- **Hearth 2.0 pipeline bar: Ready to merge replaces Merged** - The pipeline overview bar's final stage is now "Ready to merge", surfacing PRs that satisfy every mergeable condition (CI green, no pending reviews, no unresolved review threads, not conflicting, non-terminal status) so operators can see what is waiting on a human merge click. Merged PRs are no longer counted on the in-flight pipeline. The daemon's workers IPC promotes a bellows synthetic monitor's phase to `ready_to_merge` while the underlying PR meets every condition and falls it back to `bellows` the moment any flag turns red.
